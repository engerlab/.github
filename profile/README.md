# EngerLab 👋

**Physics & Engineering in Medicine**

*Towards personalized radiotherapy treatments.*

Welcome to the EngerLab GitHub organization! We are a multidisciplinary group developing personalized cancer treatment technology. Our main aim is patient-specific radiotherapy through personalized dosimetry and delivery. We also work on predicting how likely patients are to respond to a given treatment, with the goal of reducing unnecessary interventions and side effects and improving cure rates.

## 🔬 Research Areas

- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence**: AI-based dosimetry, dose optimization, and multimodality treatment outcome prediction
- 🧬 **Radiobiology and Microdosimetry**: biological effects of radiation interacting with matter
- 📡 **Detector Development**: new radiation detectors
- ☢️ **Novel Brachytherapy Technology**: new radiation sources and delivery systems

We also work on fast Monte Carlo dosimetry.

## 👥 Our Team

Our members span medical physics, radiation biology, oncology, and engineering, from B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. students to research assistants and associates.
